Fituous by Softklass - Privacy Policy



CHILDREN

Fituous is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We advise children under the age of 13 to not use this application.



DATA

We do not collect any data. The only data involved in this app is what you search for, and that is saved in the database on the device and later retrieved to populate your progress and daily stats. In addition, the only data that is stored is stored on the local database. You can delete your data by deleting the app data. No data of any kind is collected or shared.

HEALTH & SAFETY

This app is in no way shape or form providing any health recommendations. It is simply a calorie app that provides your information in a graphical way so that you can make decisions. We take no responsibility for any decisions you make based on the information provided.
